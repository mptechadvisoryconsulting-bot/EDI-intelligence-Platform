810 Invoice Implementation Guide (Sample)

Required segments and elements:
- ST01 Transaction Set = 810 (required)
- BIG02 Invoice date (required)
- BIG04 Invoice number (required)
- BIG07 Purchase order number (optional, partner-specific)
- REF*IV*02 Internal vendor number (optional)
- N1*BT*02 Bill-to name (optional)
- IT102 Quantity invoiced (required)
- IT103 Unit of measure (required)
- IT104 Unit price (required)
- TDS01 Total invoice amount (required)

Notes:
Line totals must roll up to TDS01.
Confirm whether tax and freight are separate segments or embedded in line totals.
Invoice number must be unique per trading partner relationship.
