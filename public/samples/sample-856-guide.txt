856 Ship Notice / ASN Implementation Guide (Sample)

Required segments and elements:
- ST01 Transaction Set = 856 (required)
- BSN02 Shipment identification (required)
- BSN03 Shipment date (required)
- PRF01 Purchase order number (required)
- LIN03 Buyer's part number (optional)
- SN102 Quantity shipped (required)
- SN103 Unit of measure (required)
- MAN02 Tracking number (optional, per partner)

Hierarchy notes:
HL structure typically Shipment > Order > Pack > Item (S-O-P-I).
Confirm with partner whether pack level is mandatory.

Notes:
Partial shipments must reference original PO line numbers.
Carrier SCAC and tracking rules vary by retailer.
